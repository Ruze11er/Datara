## The short version

A data analyst helps people make better decisions using data. Google's introductory course describes analysts as people who
"ask the right question; prepare, process, and analyze data for key insights; effectively share their findings with
stakeholders; and provide data-driven recommendations for thoughtful action."[^1]

In other words: **question → data → answer → action.**

## What the work looks like

Imagine you work for the online shop used throughout this app. On a normal week you might be asked:

- Which products sell the most, and which are out of stock?
- How many customers do we have in each country?
- Did more orders get cancelled this month than last month?

To answer, you pull data from the shop's database, check it is correct, calculate the numbers, and explain what they mean.

The O*NET occupation database describes business intelligence analysts, a closely related role, as people who
"produce financial and market intelligence by querying data repositories and generating periodic reports."[^2]
Among their listed tasks are generating "standard or custom reports summarizing business, financial, or economic data for review by executives"
and identifying "industry or geographic trends with business strategy implications."[^2]

*Querying data repositories* means asking databases questions, which is exactly what you will learn with SQL.

## Skills you will build in this app

| Skill | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Analytical thinking** | Turning a vague request into a clear, answerable question |

| **Statistics basics** | Knowing what an average or a trend really tells you |
| **Communication** | Explaining findings so others can act on them |

## Key takeaways

- An analyst's job is to **answer questions with data** so people can make better decisions.
- The work runs from **asking the right question** to **recommending an action**.
- Much of the daily work is **querying databases and summarizing results** in reports.
